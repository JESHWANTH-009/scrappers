### Cable Tester

To test the harness of the electrical cable upto 128 cores

The harness tester has 128 input/output programmable pins available to the user. When a test is initiated one of the pins becomes output and all others become input ports. A logic high is output on the output pin and all other pins connecting to other wires are read in. any wire that is short with the wire configured as output is read as logic high by the microcontroller.

The result will be displayed on LCD alphanumeric display. Any error will be indicated by buzzer also. A portable micro- printer is supplied along with the unit.

**Power supply :** Battery Operated

**Inputs/Outputs :** A128 pin series III connector is mounted on the unit.

**Enclosure :** Handheld ABS model.
