Hotline Interface System

### Hotline Interface System

The main objective of the system design is to interface the HOT lines coming to a control point or operations center and automatically answer the Hot line and reproduce the incoming voice on to Public Address System.

Basically this system consists of MASTER SET and SUBSCRIBER UNIT

### Specifications of Master Unit

### Inputs

- No of inputs 32 Standard PSTN lines
- Answering mode Auto answering on incoming ring
- Type of mixing Analog
- Mode of disconnection Manual

### Outputs

- No of outputs 32 Standard PSTN lines
- O/P power 10dBm in to 600E line
- Power to Internal speaker 1W
- Power to external Speaker 15W@100V
