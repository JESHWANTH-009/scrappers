LC-Launcher Test-JIG

### LC-Launcher Test-JIG

The LC-Launcher test Jig consists of industrial PC having 2no’s 8 channel serial cards, 2no’s 96 channel digital I/O cards , level converter and power supply in the 19” rack. IN this Industrial PC one serial card and digital I/O card are used for simulating the LC side computer signals. Second serial card and digital I/O card are used for simulating launcher side signals. It also test other signals like control signals and reset signals between two stations.

### General

- 19” rack
- Industrial PC
- Level converter provides the Isolation
- Power supply 32V/5A

### Inputs

- 8Nos. RS422 compatible serial channels
- 48 no’s Digital signals ( 24 No’s 28V DC/Open, 16 No’s with differential TTL )

### Outputs

- 8Nos. RS422 compatible serial channels
- 48 no’s Digital signals
- 24 No’s 28V DC/Open level converter
- 16 No’s with differential TTL
