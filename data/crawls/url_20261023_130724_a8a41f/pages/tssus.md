Time Synchronization System Using Satcom

### Time Synchronization System Using Satcom

The main objective of the system design is to synchronize various network timing systems used by PGAD located at different places, with accuracy better than 2 micro seconds with reference to a reliable standard timing source.

The system design also aims at providing time synchronization at each location with in 25 microseconds from the reference, even when the reference is not available for more than 4 hours after initial synchronization period of 1 hour.

### Specifications of SLTG

### Inputs

- IRIG-B : IRIG-B data received from the satellite with level compatible to RS232 or RS422 or TTL.
- 1PPS : 1PPS signal received from Internal GPS receiver.

### Outputs

- 1PPS Optically isolated 1PPS signal received from internal 12 CH GPS receiver or derived from reference with a level compatible to RS232/422 on our channels.
- IRIG-B DC Optically isolated and path delay compensated (or time advanced) IRIG-B DC signal on four multimode fiber optic links (850nm, 9.2 dB link budget, ST connectors)
- Power
- Series-3 D38999 – 6 Pin connector
- 230V AC +/- 10 % single-phase 50HZ +/- 3Hz or 28V+/-4V DC. Both are separately fused
- Indicators
- Separate indicators for AC and DC power availability
- GPS availability
- Transmission and Reception of the IRIG-B DC signal
- Enclosure 2U, 19” standard rack mountable unit
